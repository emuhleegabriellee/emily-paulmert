Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

After the Scroll is a product research initiative operated by Emily Paulmert in Ontario, Canada. This policy describes the intended operation of our research website and integrations. It should be reviewed and updated before enabling new data collection or customer-facing services.

Information and purpose

Our research tools are intended to analyze product listings, trend indicators and related business information from authorized data sources, including Pinterest where access is granted. We use such information to evaluate market interest, product suitability and potential ecommerce offerings. This informational website does not intentionally request visitor accounts, payment details or contact-form submissions.

Third-party services

We may use GitHub Pages to host this site and authorized third-party APIs to conduct research. These providers may process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Pinterest data, if accessed, will be handled subject to Pinterest's developer terms and applicable permissions.

Data retention and sharing

Research records may be retained for comparison and auditing. We do not intend to sell personal information collected through this website. We may disclose information when required by law or to service providers needed for operation, subject to applicable requirements.

Your choices

For privacy inquiries or requests concerning information held by this project, please email shopafterthescroll@proton.me.

Updates

We may update this policy as the research project develops. The effective date above will change when the policy is materially revised.
